# Purchase refunds

Use this glossary for course purchase refunds throughout the app and its communications.

## Language

**Refund**:
Money returned to a learner for a course purchase. Submitting or approving a refund request does not itself mean the money has been returned.
_Avoid_: Reversal, chargeback, purchase cancellation as synonyms for a refund.

**Refund request**:
A learner’s request to receive a refund for a course purchase, subject to review. A pending refund request awaits a decision; it does not confirm that a refund has been approved or paid.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, reversal, chargeback as synonyms for a refund request.
